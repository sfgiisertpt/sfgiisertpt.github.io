Star and Planet Formation Group
IISER Tirupati

The Star and Planet Formation Group (SFG@IISERT) is dedicated to advancing our understanding of stellar and planetary origins through rigorous multi-wavelength research and observation. We are also passionate about training the next generation of Indian astrophysicists and sharing the excitement of space science with the public through outreach.
